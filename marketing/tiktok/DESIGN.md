# StackGeist Short-Form Video Identity

## Style Prompt

A dense terminal/operator interface translated into vertical motion: near-black technical surfaces, thin violet rules, phosphor-green status cues, precise metadata, and large fit-first guidance. It should feel like a field console for desk and gaming setup decisions—not a glossy lifestyle ad.

## Colors

- Background: `#07050D`
- Raised surface: `#151020`
- Primary violet: `#A855F7`
- Light violet: `#C084FC`
- Primary text: `#F7F3FB`
- Muted text: `#A99FAF`
- Phosphor status: `#A3E635`
- Warning amber: `#F2B872`

## Typography

- Display and labels: Bahnschrift, heavy and tightly tracked
- Technical metadata: Cascadia Mono

## Motion

- Medium-energy directional pushes and short crossfades
- One restrained Ken Burns move per scene
- First animation begins after a brief visual hold
- Information arrives in numbered, command-like beats

## What NOT to Do

- No generic blue SaaS gradients
- No fake testimonials, fake discounts, or unsupported superlatives
- No tiny web-sized type
- No decorative glass cards or floating pills
- No frantic glitch loops that reduce readability
