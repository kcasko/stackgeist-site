# StackGeist TikTok Profile

## Recommended bio

`desk setups that fit real rooms + budgets · practical fix daily · no sponsor hype ↓`

Why: promises a reason to follow, names the audience problem, and keeps the no-hype positioning. Put `stackgeist.dev` in the website field, not the bio text, when TikTok enables the website field.

## Alternatives

1. `fit-first desk + gaming setups · buy less, fix the bottleneck · new tip daily ↓`
2. `practical gear decisions for real desks · skip reasons included · stackgeist.dev`

## Profile image

Use `marketing/tiktok/launch-v1/stackgeist-profile-photo.png`. It is already rendered for the terminal/operator identity. Avoid tiny text; the STACK GEIST wordmark is readable at avatar size.

## Pin strategy

Pin three videos once available:
1. Brand promise: "upgrade your desk in this order" — broadest fit-first thesis.
2. Highest-performing practical fix after 7 days (do not guess before analytics).
3. Best comparison video — likely USB-C cable or hub, but choose by saves/completion rate.

## Testing rule

Keep two broad tags (`#tech`, `#productivity`) and two niche tags (`#desksetup` plus topic tag) stable for seven posts. Rotate one experimental tag. Judge by completion rate and saves, not views alone.
