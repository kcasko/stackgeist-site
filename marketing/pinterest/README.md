# StackGeist Pinterest Launch

## Profile
**Display name:** StackGeist | Gaming & Desk Setups  
**Website:** https://stackgeist.dev  
**Bio:** Gaming setups, desk upgrades, cable management, lighting, and useful gear. Practical ideas for better spaces without the clutter.

## Launch boards
- Gaming Setup Ideas
- Small Space Gaming
- Budget Desk Setups
- Cable Management
- Desk Setup Guides

## Creative system
- 1000 x 1500 px (2:3)
- Realistic setup imagery dominates the frame
- Near-black / graphite / deep violet / selective electric purple
- One clear headline, one optional support line, small StackGeist wordmark
- No fake UI, fake telemetry, decorative numbering, glassmorphism, card grids, or excessive neon
- Do not depict a specific product unless the product is actually the subject and the visual is accurate

## Launch cadence
Publish one Pin per day for the first 10 days. Do not publish both creative variants for the same article back-to-back. Separate variants by several days so the feed does not look repetitive.

## Destination strategy
Pins link to StackGeist editorial pages with UTM parameters. Affiliate links remain inside the relevant article/recommendation page where the disclosure and buying context live.

## First optimization loop
After the first 10 Pins have had time to collect data, compare impressions, saves, outbound clicks, and outbound CTR by creative angle. Make the next batch from the winning headline + visual patterns rather than increasing volume blindly.
