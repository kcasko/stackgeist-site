The site sets headlines in HTML. **Images must not contain titles, slogans, slash bars, or logos.** Live text is sharper and searchable, and it can change without regenerating the image.

## Rules for every image

- **No text, logos, or brand marks.** That covers screens, book spines, mugs, and stickers. Turn screens off, blur them, or angle them away.
- **No real product likeness** when the page reviews that product. Use generic hardware, or a real photo of the actual unit.
- **No software UI.** Exact interfaces (Windows, ADUC, AWS, VMware) come from real screenshots.
- Use believable, lived-in desks and workbenches with natural window or desk-lamp light. Avoid neon, RGB glow, and cinematic orange-and-teal grading.
- Vary the camera angle between images: overhead, 45°, low and close, wide.
- **Export at full size** (sizes below) as PNG or high-quality WebP. Don't down-res first.
