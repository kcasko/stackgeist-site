# Product imagery and evidence

The November 6 audit found product images copied from Amazon searches and manufacturer pages without a documented reuse permission. Attribution alone is not permission. Those 145 public files were checksum-backed up outside this repository before removal from the served tree. `image-provenance-2026-11-06.json` records each original path, hash, replacement and local recovery path.

The affected pages remain available. They now use existing StackGeist-authored generic artwork, captioned as an illustration rather than a photograph of the model. Old image paths temporarily redirect to that artwork. Generic artwork is not offered as product imagery in Product schema.

Existing Commons examples retain their source, credit, license link and example-photo label. They are not claimed to show the author's own unit. Validate the source identity and license before adding or replacing an asset.

To restore a real product photo, record the exact source, author/rightsholder, license or written permission, permitted uses, required attribution and date checked. For owned hardware, use a real photograph and keep ownership separate from performance or laboratory-test claims. Do not rerun the retired Amazon image scraping workflow.
